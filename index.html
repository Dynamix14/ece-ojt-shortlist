Where the numbers come from

  • ₱ figures are lifted from the posting itself — Prosple's salary field for Security Bank STAR, an Indeed salary line for the Coca-Cola Santa Rosa IE intern. Not estimates I invented.
  • Distance is OSRM road distance from DLSU–Taft (14.5649, 120.9931), traffic-free. Add 40–100% at rush hour.
  • Intern reality quotes Indeed and JobStreet intern reviews. “No first-hand account” means exactly that.
  • Allowances shift per cohort and per business unit — the number in a 2025 posting is not a promise for 2027.

Ask these three questions

  • Is there a monthly allowance, and is it released weekly or at the end?
  • Does it cover transport for site/plant visits, or is that separate?
  • Which unit will I be assigned to? At Security Bank and Coca-Cola the answer changes the work entirely.
